first tunable entry targeting an INACTIVE port slice:
  apcie-phy-ip-auspma-tunables entry #30: offset=0x12000 -> target_addr=0x497052000
If m1n1's tunable applicator hits this address, the AXI
write to an unpowered slave hangs the fabric silently.
This matches the observed wedge in pcie_up_2.log.
